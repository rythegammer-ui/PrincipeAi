### 1.4 Postiz: let Claude schedule the calendar

**What it does.** This workspace has a Postiz skill. **Once you create a Postiz account and provide an API key, Claude can schedule the calendar through the Postiz skill:** it drafts each week's posts from the post kits with the right tagged links, uploads the images and videos, and schedules them across your channels. Claude can also read Postiz's analytics for your Monday scorecard.

**Which plan.** Your Tier 1 and Tier 2 channels are Instagram, Facebook Page, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn (you), LinkedIn Page, Threads, X and Pinterest. That's 9 channels. Bluesky makes 10, so **Team** (10 channels) fits exactly. Reddit stays manual: you only comment there for the first 3 weeks.

**Steps (Recommendation):**

1. **Start the 7-day trial on L-8 (Mon Nov 2),** once every Tier 1 profile exists. Keep the warm-up (Nov 4-9; nothing on Election Day, Tue Nov 3) queued in the native tools while you test. Decide by L-2 (Sun Nov 8), before the trial ends, so that on L-1 (Mon Nov 9) you schedule launch week and week 2 in the tool you keep.
2. In Postiz, connect each channel. Each platform asks you to log in and approve access.
3. In Postiz's settings, create an API key. Where the setting sits is **UNVERIFIED**, so check in the app.
4. Give the key to Claude, ideally as a **secret environment variable** named `POSTIZ_API_KEY` in your Claude environment's settings rather than pasted into a chat. Never put it in a repo file, a shared doc or a public post. Anyone with the key can post as you. Revoke it in Postiz if it leaks.
5. Every week, Claude creates the posts as **drafts**. You approve them in Postiz. Claude then schedules the approved ones at the calendar's times (Eastern Time).

**Know before you rely on it:**
- **TikTok:** a post sent with the "upload" method lands in your TikTok inbox unpublished, and Postiz still reports success. Claude uses direct posting unless you ask otherwise. Check your first TikTok goes live.
- **Sounds:** post any Reel or TikTok that needs a trending sound from your phone (see the hypothesis in 1.2).
- **Replies:** Postiz doesn't do your replying. Answer comments and DMs in each app.
- **No time to set it up?** Skip it. The $0 stack works for launch, and you can switch later.

### 1.6 When to upgrade, and what to skip

**Upgrade when (Rule of thumb):**
- **Buffer's queue is full.** Buffer Free holds 10 scheduled posts per channel at a time [S1]. Two weeks of daily X posts is 14, so you can't batch two weeks ahead. Either batch one week at a time or move to Postiz or Buffer Essentials.
- **Metricool's cap bites.** Its free plan allows 20 posts a month [S2].
- **Copying posts costs you an hour a week.** If you paste the same post into three or more schedulers and that takes more than about an hour a week, Postiz pays for itself in time.
- **You need Magic Resize, background removal or your own fonts.** That's when Canva Pro is worth it [S43].
- **Keyword DMs outgrow Meta's tool,** or you want DM automation on TikTok. Then try ManyChat [S41][S42].

**Skip for now:**
- **Hootsuite and Later.** Too expensive for what you need [S3][S5].
- **Meta One.** It's Meta's new paid subscription, announced 2026-09-15 [S62]. Clickable links in organic Instagram posts and Reels come only on paid tiers, from $49.99 a month (reported; Meta says features vary by region) [S73][S74]. Free Facebook Pages are reportedly limited to about **2 organic link posts a month** [S73]. Read Meta's help page "About links in organic Facebook Page posts and comments" inside your Page to see your Page's limit (**UNVERIFIED**). Until then, use the Page's action button, the Website field, comment keywords and first-comment links.
- **X Premium.** Only if X starts bringing leads (see rule 9 in 3.4).
- **Linktree and similar tools.** `/links` already does the job and keeps attribution intact (2.6).

### 2.2 Rules that keep the data clean

- **Main bio link everywhere:** `https://principeai.com/links?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=evergreen&utm_content=bio`. Platforms that allow several links also get direct links to `/free-guide`, `/assessment`, a free preview lesson and `/courses`, all with `utm_medium=bio`. Every profile link is listed in [profile setup](01-profile-setup.md), section 8.
- **Starter Kit links always go to `/free-guide`, never `/guide`.**
- **Never link a private page:** `/assessment/results/...`, `/booking/...` or `/book/confirmed/...`.
- **Lowercase, no spaces, hyphens only.** Funnels lowercases `utm_source` before grouping, but the CSV export and the contact page show exactly what you typed (Repo: `src/lib/funnels.ts`, `src/app/api/admin/contacts/export/route.ts`).
- **Copy links. Never retype them.** One wrong letter in `utm_source` makes a new row in Admin → Funnels.
- **If the link has a `#section`, the tags go before the `#`:** `.../courses/ai-for-business?utm_source=youtube&...&utm_content=lc-12#enroll`.
- **Don't tag links between your own profiles** (your Instagram listed on YouTube) **or links inside the site.** A tag on an internal link would wrongly credit a visitor who arrived untagged.
- **Don't use `utm_term`.** The site stores it, but nothing reports on it.
- **DM replies lose the post ID.** All KIT replies use `dm-kit`, whichever post triggered them. That's the price of one automation per keyword, and it's fine at launch.
- **Some links can't be clicked.** URLs in YouTube Shorts descriptions and comments aren't clickable [S68], so put tagged links in the channel links and long-form descriptions. Instagram captions aren't clickable without a paid Meta One plan [S73][S74], so use the bio, Story stickers and keyword DMs.
- **DM keywords:** KIT → `/free-guide` (`dm-kit`); READY → `/assessment` (`dm-ready`); WORKFLOW → `/courses/ai-automation-accelerator/preview/anatomy-of-an-ai-workflow` (`dm-workflow`). The matching comment hashtags are `#prompts`, `#aiready` and `#aiworkflow`.

**Old values to fix on sight.** Early drafts and research used values that are now retired. If you see one in a draft, replace it before posting:

| Retired | Use instead |
|---|---|
| `utm_campaign=link-in-bio`, `profile`, `starter-kit`, `assessment` | `evergreen` |
| `launch-2026`, `bfcm-2026`, `newyear-2027` | `launch`, `bf-2026`, `ny-2027` |
| `utm_source=email`, `partner-<name>` | `newsletter` or `partner`, with the name in `utm_content` |
| `utm_medium=video` | `social` |
| `utm_medium=social` on podcast, partner, local or newsletter links | `referral` |
| `utm_medium=social` on DM replies | `dm` |
| `utm_content=bio-starter-kit`, `bio-strategy-call`, `page-button`, `profile`, date-style IDs (`20261103-reel-...`) | `bio-kit`, `bio-call`, `button`, `bio`, the post ID |

### 2.6 How the site records a visit (first touch, 90 days)

Repo: `src/proxy.ts`, `src/lib/tracking.ts`, `src/lib/crm.ts`.

1. **The first tagged click wins.** When someone opens any page with `utm_` tags, the site saves those tags in a cookie called `pa_utm` for **90 days**. It does this only if that browser doesn't already hold one.
2. **Later clicks don't change it.** Tagged or not, nothing replaces the cookie until it expires. After 90 days, the next tagged click starts a new one.
3. **The tags move onto the contact when they give their email.** That happens at the Starter Kit opt-in, the assessment, a booking, the AI chat, the contact form, an application or checkout. If that email is already a contact, the old record **keeps its original tags forever**.
4. **Bookings and sales are credited to the contact's tags**, whenever they happen. Someone who opted in from TikTok in November and buys in January counts as a TikTok sale in January.
5. **The first referring site and first landing page** are saved too, from that browser's very first visit. The landing page includes the full tagged URL.

**What this means in practice:**
- Someone taps your TikTok bio link, then three weeks later taps a LinkedIn post link and opts in. TikTok gets the credit.
- **Every browser is separate.** The Instagram and TikTok in-app browsers, Safari and a laptop each have their own cookies (widely reported for in-app browsers, **UNVERIFIED** against Meta's docs). If someone opts in inside Instagram's browser, they stay credited to Instagram even if they buy on a laptop later, as long as they use the same email.
- Someone who arrives without tags (typed the address, an untagged link) and signs up shows as **Direct / none**. That's why every link you control carries tags.
- **Paid results are undercounted.** A person who clicked an organic link in the last 90 days keeps that credit even if a boost brings them back (5.5).
- Platform numbers and site numbers never match. "Link taps" in Instagram won't equal visitors in Admin → Funnels. Trust the site for leads and revenue, and the platforms for reach.
- The privacy policy already lists the `pa_utm`, `pa_ref` and `pa_land` cookies (Repo: `src/app/(marketing)/privacy/page.tsx`). No new disclosure is needed.

### 3.4 Decision rules

These are starting rules, not industry benchmarks. Every number below compares you with **your own** results. Change the rules once you know your data.

**First, set your baselines.** For each platform and format (Reels, carousels, Shorts and so on), your baseline is the **median** views of your last 10 posts, or of all of them if you have fewer. Use the median, not the average: one breakout post drags an average up. In the Posts tab, sort by platform, format and date, then take the median of that group's last 10 "Views at 7 days" cells, for example `=MEDIAN(G2:G11)`.

1. **Winner.** A post with **at least 2× its baseline views**, or any post that brought a lead (its ID appears as UTM content on a new contact). Rule of thumb.
2. **Double down.** When a format or hook produces a winner twice in 3 weeks, make 2 more next week: same format, new topic. Cut a version for each other Tier 1 platform.
3. **Retire.** A format that sits in its platform's bottom 20% three weeks running, with no leads, takes a month off.
4. **One experiment a week.** Change one thing only: the hook, length, time slot or CTA keyword. Write it in the weekly log.
5. **Don't judge a platform before Mon Dec 7 (L+27).** That's too little data, and launch week skews everything. The only exceptions are a restricted account or a broken link.
6. **Checkpoint, Mon Dec 7 (L+27).** A Tier 1 platform has had at least 3 posts a week for 4 weeks, brought zero leads, and its weekly views are flat or falling? Cut it back to cross-posts only (Tier 2 effort) through the holidays.
7. **Full review, Mon Feb 1, 2027 (L+83).** Admin → Funnels → 90 days. Rank channels by leads, then by booked calls and revenue. Divide each by the hours spent (table C).
   - Keep the top 2-3 as Tier 1.
   - Move the rest to Tier 2.
   - Promote any Tier 2 or Tier 3 platform that beat a Tier 1 platform on leads.
8. **Never delete an account or let a handle go.** A platform you drop keeps its bio link and gets cross-posts.
9. **X stays Tier 2 unless it brings leads.** In Buffer's study, free accounts' median reach was under 100 impressions a post, against over 600 for Premium accounts. The link is a correlation: people who pay may also post more [S76]. Consider paying for Premium only if X shows leads at the Dec 7 checkpoint. The price is **UNVERIFIED** after the reported October tier change [S8].
10. **Leads beat views.** A platform with fewer views and more leads wins.
11. **Spending money** follows section 5.

### 4.3 Endorsements and testimonials (FTC Endorsement Guides, revised 2023)

- **Testimonials must reflect typical results.** "Results not typical" or "Individual results may vary" doesn't fix an atypical testimonial. You need proof that the result is typical, or you must "clearly and conspicuously disclose the generally expected performance" [S22].
- **The disclosure must be visible without tapping "more".** Platform tools alone aren't automatically enough [S22].
- **Disclose material connections:** affiliate commissions, free products or perks, and employment. "Listing your employer on your profile page isn't enough" [S22].
- **What this means for Principe AI today:** there are **no testimonials**, so never write or imply any. No "students say...", no invented DMs, no AI-written quotes. The site's testimonial sections stay hidden while `site.testimonials` is empty (Repo: `src/config/site.ts`).
- **After launch:**
  - Collect real feedback, with written permission to quote it.
  - If someone got free or discounted access in exchange for feedback, say so next to their quote, for example "received free course access".
  - When results vary, say what's typical.
  - If a partner earns a referral fee, they must disclose it. If a podcast charges you to appear, that's an ad, and it must be disclosed.

### 4.4 Fake reviews and fake influence (16 CFR Part 465, in force since 2024-10-21)

The rule bans [S23][S24][S25]:
- Fake or false reviews and testimonials, **including AI-generated ones**, and any from someone without real experience.
- Paying for reviews on condition that they're positive (or negative).
- Insider reviews (staff, family) without a disclosed connection.
- Company-run "independent" review sites.
- Suppressing negative reviews.
- **Buying or selling fake indicators of social media influence:** fake followers, views or likes.

**Penalties:** up to **$53,088 per violation** for knowing violations (the 2025 figure; the FTC's amounts are unchanged for 2026) [S27]. The FTC sent a round of warning letters under this rule in Dec 2025 [S26].

**Practical rules:**
- Never buy followers, views or engagement, including "starter packs" for new accounts. No engagement pods.
- Friends and family don't post reviews without saying who they are.
- Never tie a course discount to a 5-star review.

### 4.5 Earnings and outcome claims (the highest risk in this category)

- **Operation AI Comply** (Sept 2024) went after schemes claiming AI tools would produce passive income [S28]. The FTC also acted against **Lurn** (Sept 2023), an online business-coaching programme, over money-making claims [S30]. **Air AI** was charged in Aug 2025 with deceptive claims about "business growth, earnings potential, and refund guarantees". Its March 2026 settlement bans its owners from marketing business opportunities [S29].
- **Pending rule:** in Jan 2025 the FTC proposed extending the Business Opportunity Rule to "money-making opportunities", including **business coaching**. Earnings claims would need written proof that they reflect typical buyers' results [S31]. Whether it has been finalised is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **What this means for Principe AI (Recommendation):**
  - **Don't claim** income, revenue, "10x", clients won or specific hours saved without written proof. "Grow with AI" is fine as a tagline. "Add $5k a month with AI" is not.
  - Show the process, not a promised outcome. For example: "Here's the 3-step workflow I use to draft client replies. How much time it saves depends on your volume."
  - Keep a proof file: screenshots, before-and-after timings of your own work labelled as yours, and the source of every statistic you quote.
  - No "guaranteed". Never promise a "money-back guarantee". On refunds, `/terms` says "We review every request individually and fairly" (Repo: `src/app/(marketing)/terms/page.tsx`). Any refund wording must match `/terms` exactly; refund-guarantee claims were part of the Air AI charges [S29].

## 5. Paid boosting

### 5.1 Don't spend yet if any of these is true

**Recommendation.**
- **The CAN-SPAM address isn't fixed** (4.1), or the Starter Kit emails aren't running.
- **Your links aren't proven.** `/links` and `/free-guide` aren't live with tags, or the link test (2.8) hasn't passed.
- **The free page is leaking.** The Free Guide funnel's biggest drop is "Visited → Opted in" and you haven't tried to fix the page yet. Paying to send people to a leaky page wastes money.
- **You haven't posted consistently for at least 4 weeks.** That means fewer than about 20 posts to pick a winner from.
- **No post has beaten its own baseline by 2-3×** without paid help (3.4). Boost proven posts only, never a new one.
- **It's Black Friday week.** Ad prices are commonly reported to spike from Black Friday week to Cyber Monday, around Nov 23 to Nov 30 (**Hypothesis**, **UNVERIFIED**). A brand-new account shouldn't run its first test then.

### 5.2 When

| Window | L-day | Dates | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before the Dec 7 checkpoint | Before L+27 | Before Mon Dec 7 | No |
| Thanksgiving to Cyber Monday | L+16 to L+20 | Thu Nov 26 to Mon Nov 30 | No (5.1) |
| Early December | L+27 to L+38 | Mon Dec 7 to Fri Dec 18 | **Possible first test**, if every condition in 5.1 is met. Tag it `evergreen` |
| Holiday slow-down | L+41 to L+52 | Dec 21 to Jan 1 | No: you're posting lightly |
| New Year push | L+53 to L+65 | Sat Jan 2 to Thu Jan 14 | **Best first test.** Boost a December winner with the "build a 30-day AI habit" angle. Tag it `ny-2027` |

### 5.3 What to boost

- **One post on one platform at a time.** Pick the platform that already sends you the most leads (table B).
- **A post that already won** (3.4, rule 1) and points to the **Starter Kit** at `/free-guide`. A free offer is the easiest first step for a cold audience. Don't make your first boost a course sales post.
- **No prices, claims, testimonials or "you"-attribute copy** in boosted posts (4.5, 4.12).
- **Instagram:** a feed post or carousel, or a Reel that was **not** shared to Facebook. Instagram Help says a boostable Reel must be 90 seconds or less, 9:16, with no licensed music or interactive stickers, and Reels already shared to Facebook can't be boosted [S80]. Reels auto-shared to the Page ([profile setup](01-profile-setup.md), 4.1) can't be boosted on Instagram, so boost the Facebook copy from the Page, or re-post the winning Reel Instagram-only first. A Story with a link sticker can't be boosted [S69].
- **TikTok:** content disclosure on, CML music only (4.7, 4.10).
- **LinkedIn:** only a **Company Page** post that has outperformed your other Page posts.
- **Goal:** choose "website visits" (Meta) or "More website visits" (TikTok Promote [S53]), not followers or engagement.
- **Tag the boost:** `utm_medium=paid_social`, the campaign of that window, and the post ID as `utm_content`. Example: `https://principeai.com/free-guide?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=ny-2027&utm_content=vc-02`.

### 5.5 Track it and decide

1. **Before you spend,** write down the most you'll pay for one new Starter Kit subscriber. That's your own limit, not a benchmark.
2. **During the test,** check Admin → Contacts every day for new contacts with UTM medium `paid_social`. **Stop early** if you've spent $20 with zero such leads (Rule of thumb).
3. **After the test,** cost per lead = money spent ÷ new contacts with `utm_medium=paid_social` (CSV export, `utm_medium` column).
4. **Keep boosting** only if the cost per lead is at or below your limit. Otherwise try a different winning post once, then stop paid tests for a month.
5. **Remember the undercount:** people who had already clicked one of your organic links in the last 90 days stay credited to that link (2.6). Count the boost's link clicks in the ad tool too.
6. **Once you have 30+ leads and some sales,** work out what a lead is worth from your own data. Take every lead from the last 90 days in the CSV, add up `lifetime_value_usd`, and divide by the number of leads. Use that to set your next limit.
