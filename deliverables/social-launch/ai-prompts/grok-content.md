# Grok prompts: Principe AI content

> **What this is:** two prompts to paste into Grok. Prompt 1 writes posts and short-video scripts. Prompt 2 makes background visuals in Grok Imagine; add the words yourself in Canva. Both carry the brand facts and the rules from the [content bank](../07-content-bank.md), section 6, so Grok doesn't invent stats, testimonials or prices.
>
> **Before posting anything Grok writes:** check every fact against the site, and leave off any CTA that depends on something that isn't live yet (the comment-keyword DMs go live on Oct 28). Where a platform asks whether content is AI-generated, answer honestly.

## Prompt 1: posts and short-video scripts

```text
You're my social media content writer for Principe AI, my AI education business. Write 7 posts for this week: 3 short-video scripts (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts), 2 Instagram carousels, 1 LinkedIn post and 1 X post.

ABOUT PRINCIPE AI (use only these facts; never invent others)
- Principe AI, run by Principe Consults. Tagline: "Learn AI. Implement AI. Grow with AI."
- Practical AI education and implementation for business owners, professionals and everyday people: self-paced courses, 1:1 coaching and done-with-you AI systems, in plain English.
- How it works: Assess → Learn → Implement.
- Free AI Starter Kit (principeai.com/free-guide): 50 copy-paste prompts + a 7-day plan, about 10 minutes a day. No card required, nothing to cancel. Works with the free versions of AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini. The 7 days: 1 Meet your AI assistant · 2 Learn the 5-part prompt · 3 Clear a message you've been avoiding · 4 Plan something real · 5 Learn something faster · 6 Make a clearer decision · 7 Build your AI toolkit.
- Free AI Readiness Assessment (principeai.com/assessment): about 3 minutes, instant results: a readiness score out of 100, a tier (Explorer, Experimenter, Implementer or Scaler), a personalized AI action plan and a recommended next step.
- Free 30-minute AI Strategy Call: principeai.com/book/ai-strategy-call
- Courses: AI Essentials (beginner, everyday life), AI for Business Owners (intermediate), AI Automation Accelerator (advanced: workflows and simple agents, mostly without code). Don't mention prices.
- The 5-part prompt: Role, Context, Task, Format, Examples. Context is the part most people skip.
- Our rule: "AI drafts, people decide."

TOPICS TO DRAW FROM
Everyday AI wins (emails, planning, learning, decisions), prompt skills, where AI actually pays off in a small business, keeping customer data safe, why AI makes things up and how to check it, and automations without the hype.

VOICE
Confident, warm, plain English, practical, no hype, American spelling. Teach one useful thing per post that people can try today. Write like a helpful friend who knows AI, not a guru.

HARD RULES
- No made-up statistics, percentages, studies, testimonials, student numbers, ratings, "trusted by", or claims about time or money saved.
- No income or results promises, no "guaranteed", no "money-back guarantee".
- No prices, discounts or deadlines.
- Name AI tools only as "AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini". Never imply Principe AI is affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic, OpenAI or Google.
- No personal stories or credentials for me. If a post needs one, write [YOUR STORY] as a placeholder.
- Calls to action: "Start free at principeai.com" or "link in bio". Don't use "comment a keyword" or "DM me" CTAs.
- Any prompt you include must be complete, copy-paste ready, with [BRACKETS] for what the reader fills in.
- If you're not sure something is true, leave it out and tell me.

FORMAT FOR EACH POST
- Platform and format
- Hook (the first line or first 2 seconds), under 12 words
- For videos: a script of about 30-45 seconds with [ON SCREEN TEXT] cues; for carousels: 7-9 slides, one idea per slide, under 30 words each
- Caption (Instagram up to 5 hashtags; TikTok up to 4; LinkedIn up to 3; X 280 characters max, no hashtags)
- CTA
At the end, list any facts you used that I should double-check.
```

## Prompt 2: background visuals (Grok Imagine)

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 background image for an AI education brand called Principe AI. Style: clean, modern, premium, abstract. A smooth gradient from deep violet (#4a1fb8) to royal violet (#6938ef), with soft glowing light and one or two large, soft four-pointed sparkle shapes in warm gold (#fec84b), slightly blurred, off to one side. Gentle depth and subtle grain, plenty of calm empty space in the middle third for text to be added later.
No text, letters or numbers. No logos or brand marks of any company. No people, faces or hands. No robots, brains, circuit boards or glowing blue "AI" clichés. Not photorealistic.
```

Variations: change "vertical 9:16" to "portrait 4:5" for feed posts, and swap the description for "a dark ink background (#0c0a1d) with a soft violet glow at the top right and a faint gold glow at the bottom left" for a darker set. For a moving background, ask for "a slow, seamless 5-second loop: the glow drifts gently and the sparkles shimmer", same rules.
