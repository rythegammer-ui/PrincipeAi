## 1. Before you start (15 minutes)

**Gear:** your phone on a tripod, a clip-on mic if you have one (otherwise film close to the phone in a quiet room), and a laptop or second phone for the teleprompter and screen recordings.

1. **Light:** face a window. In the evening, put one lamp or LED light at 45° to one side, slightly above eye level, and turn off the ceiling lights.
2. **Camera:** vertical, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Rear camera if you can see your framing, otherwise the front camera. Lens at eye level, about an arm's length away, framed from mid-chest up.
3. **Keep the edges clear:** nothing important in the top 15% or bottom 25% of the frame, or near the right edge. That's where the app buttons sit.
4. **Background:** tidy, nothing private on walls or screens. Something violet or gold in shot is a nice brand touch.
5. **Quiet:** fans off, phone on Do Not Disturb. Record 5 seconds of silence first; your editor uses it to smooth cuts.
6. **Test:** film 10 seconds, play it back with headphones, check the sound and framing.
7. **Teleprompter:** paste each script below into a free teleprompter app (for example "Teleprompter Premium" or "PromptSmart", free tiers), or read one line, look up, and say it.

## 4. Screen recordings to make

Record these on your phone with the built-in screen recorder (Do Not Disturb on). Use a **private browser window**, so your own visits don't mix into your tracking.

| For | What to record |
|---|---|
| LD-01 | Scroll `principeai.com` slowly: the hero, "For your life" and "For your business", then "Assess · Learn · Implement" |
| LD-02 | Scroll `principeai.com/free-guide`: the headline, the 7-day plan titles and the prompt categories |
| LD-02 | In a **fresh chat** in an AI assistant, paste the prompt below with made-up notes. Leave the prompt on screen for 2 seconds, then let the answer appear. Record it twice and keep the better take |
| LD-03 | Take `principeai.com/assessment` with a **test email address** (for example a `+test` version of your email). Tap through a few questions, then show your results page with the **address bar hidden**. Delete the test contact in Admin afterwards |

**The LD-02 prompt** (fill in the brackets with made-up details before you record):
```
Turn these rough notes into a clear, friendly email to [RECIPIENT]:
[YOUR NOTES OR BULLET POINTS]
Keep it under [NUMBER] words, put the most important point first, and end with a clear next step.
```

**Rules for AI screen recordings:** real answers only; trim the waiting, but never change the answer's text. If you speed it up, add "sped up 2×" on screen. Never type real names, client details or account numbers.
