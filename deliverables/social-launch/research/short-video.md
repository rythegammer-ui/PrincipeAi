## 2. Cross-platform fundamentals (apply everywhere)

### 2.1 What the ranking systems reward in 2026

- **Instagram (officially):** the most important Reels predictions are whether a viewer will reshare it, watch it to the end, like it, and visit its audio page [V·S23]. Mosseri named watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as the top three signals. Sends matter slightly more for reaching non-followers [R·S28].
- **Facebook:** prioritises original Reels. Views and watch time of original Reels "approximately doubled" in the second half of 2025 [V·S50]. It is also testing a post-view "how well does this match your interests" survey [R·S55].
- **TikTok:** "Content is also ineligible for the FYF if it includes unoriginal or reused material without anything new" [V·S8]. Fake engagement, or "content that tries to manipulate people into… inflating likes or follows", is also ineligible for the For You feed [V·S8].
- **YouTube Shorts:** the creator-facing retention metric is **"Viewed vs. swiped away"** in Studio [R·S47]. Monetisation counts engaged views [R·S47].

**What this means for Principe AI.** Teach one useful thing that a viewer would *send to a friend or colleague* ("send this to the person who still writes emails from scratch"). On every platform, sends and shares are the strongest route to people who don't follow you yet.

### 2.2 Hooks and retention (practical rules)

No platform publishes a retention threshold. TikTok's ad guidance tells advertisers to land the main idea in the first 3 seconds and the hook within 6 seconds. The widely quoted "63% of top-CTR ads show the key message in 3 s" figure could not be traced to a live TikTok page (UNVERIFIED) [R·S17].

1. **Frame 1 = the payoff.** Show the finished result (the polished email, the filled-in SOP, the automation running) *before* explaining. No logo sting, no "Hey guys, today…".
2. **Say the searchable phrase in the first sentence**, and put it on screen as a title, e.g. "ChatGPT prompt for follow-up emails". This is the hook *and* the search keyword (2.3).
3. **On-screen text for the first 2 seconds** (top third, clear of the UI). Most people watch with the sound off at first.
4. **Cut dead air.** Jump-cut every pause longer than about 0.3 s. Zoom the screen recording to the part that matters, because UI text is unreadable at phone size.
5. **Leave the prompt on screen long enough to screenshot** (1.5–2 s, or a pause card). This drives saves and sends, and the CTA can be "the full version is in the free kit".
6. **Loop-friendly ending.** End on a line that leads back into the opening, and don't add a 5-second outro. On Shorts and TikTok, replays add to watch time.
7. **One CTA per video**, at the very end or in the pinned comment. Never ask for likes or follows as a condition ("follow for part 2 or you'll miss it" is borderline under TikTok's fake-engagement wording [V·S8]).

### 2.3 Search is now the second front door

- **Instagram (official tips):** use a handle and **profile name** related to your content, because search matches text. Put keywords in the bio. "Put keywords and hashtags in the caption, not the comments" [V·S24].
- Public professional-account content can show up in **Google** [V·S20], so the first caption line works like a Google title.
- **TikTok:** Creator Search Insights shows what people search for, with a "content gap" filter for searches that have few videos [V·S9]. The TikTok Studio help menu still lists it [V·S7 nav]. Third-party guides agree that TikTok indexes spoken words (auto-captions), on-screen text and captions, but TikTok publishes no weights [R].
- **YouTube:** Shorts titles and descriptions are searchable. Hashtags in Shorts descriptions are clickable [V·S40].

**Keyword pattern for Principe AI.** Use [tool] + [task] + [audience], for example "ChatGPT prompt for client follow-up emails", "Claude for small business SOPs" or "AI tools for real estate agents". Put it in three places: spoken in the first 3 s, the on-screen title, and the first caption line.

### 2.4 Originality, and posting your own video on four platforms

- Instagram: original content means "Photos or videos you took… if it's something you personally created, it's your original content." Re-uploading someone else's work with only a border, watermark, subtitles or a caption credit does **not** count [V·S25]. Separately, Instagram makes "watermarked reels" and "reels that have already been posted on Instagram" less visible [V·S23].
- Facebook: "Content filmed or produced directly by a creator or owner of a Profile or Page is considered original." Duplicates and minor edits of *another creator's* post are deprioritised [V·S50]. Meta fingerprints videos to know which account uploaded them first [R·S55].
- TikTok: unoriginal or reused material "without anything new" cannot appear in the For You feed [V·S8]. Whether TikTok down-ranks *your own* original video because it also appears elsewhere is not addressed officially (UNVERIFIED). The real risk is a visible watermark.
- YouTube: Shorts downloaded from YouTube Studio carry a YouTube watermark (2022 change) [R·S48]. The 2025 "inauthentic content" rule targets mass-produced, repetitive channels, not people posting their own footage on several platforms [R·S46].

**Workflow that stays safe on all four:**
1. Edit the master in a neutral editor (Descript, Premiere, CapCut desktop, or Instagram's Edits) and export **without** any platform watermark or end card. CapCut's free templates can add an end clip; remove it.
2. Never download a finished post from TikTok, YouTube or Instagram to re-upload it.
3. Upload natively to each platform and change the caption, keywords and CTA for each (sections 3–6).
4. Never upload the same file twice to the *same* platform. To retry an idea, re-cut it with a new hook and opening shot. On Instagram, test hooks with Trial Reels (2.6).

### 2.5 AI-content labelling: what applies to an AI educator

| What you post | TikTok [V·S7] | YouTube [V·S42] | Instagram / Facebook [V·S31] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Screen recording of ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini answering a prompt | No label | No label | No label |
| Script, outline, title, thumbnail or captions written with AI | No label | Explicitly exempt ("production assistance… captions… idea generation") | No label |
| AI-generated **realistic** b-roll (a fake office, a fake client meeting) | Label required | Disclose | Label required (photorealistic video) |
| AI avatar of the founder reading a script (HeyGen, Synthesia, etc.) | Label: "a real person… whose image, voice, and/or words are altered or modified by AI" counts as AIGC | Disclose to be safe; making a person "appear to say… something they didn't" is a disclosure trigger | Label required |
| Founder's own cloned voice as a voiceover | Label (AI-altered voice) | Exempt ("cloning one's own voice to create voice overs or dubs") | Label if the audio is realistic (it will be) |
| AI-generated music as the main track | Label | Disclose (AI music) | Realistic audio, so label |
| Cartoon or illustration-style AI graphics | Encouraged if fully AI-generated | Exempt (not realistic) | Exempt (cartoon example given) |

- Labels do not reduce reach on their own. TikTok: turning the label on "won't affect the distribution of your video" [V·S7]. YouTube: "Disclosing AI content alone won't limit a video's audience or impact its eligibility to earn money" [V·S42].
- **Auto-labels.** TikTok and YouTube auto-label files that carry C2PA Content Credentials, and those labels **cannot be removed** [V·S7][V·S42]. Many AI image and video generators embed C2PA, so a 2-second AI b-roll clip can label the whole video.
- Under-labelling is the risk. YouTube may apply labels manually, remove content or suspend YPP for creators who "consistently choose not to disclose" [V·S42]. TikTok may remove unlabelled realistic AI content [V·S7].
- Audience trend: TikTok is testing a slider that lets viewers see less AI content [R·S16]. Mosseri's January 2026 note says authenticity is becoming "infinitely reproducible" and therefore valuable [R·S28]. **A real human on camera, using real tools, is a positioning advantage for Principe AI. Don't swap it for an avatar.**

### 2.6 Testing without hurting the main account

- **Instagram Trial Reels:** shown to non-followers first and kept off your grid and followers' feeds. They can be shared to everyone later, manually or automatically if they perform. Eligibility: a public professional account with at least **200 followers** [V·S26]. Use them for A/B hook tests once you pass 200.
- **TikTok:** no equivalent was found. Re-cut losing ideas with a new hook after about 2 weeks.

### 2.7 UTM tagging for this site (matches the code in the repo)

The site stores **first-touch** UTMs in the `pa_utm` cookie for 90 days, and only on a visitor's first landing (`src/proxy.ts`, `ATTRIBUTION_TTL = 90 days`). Admin → Funnels groups channels by **lower-cased `utm_source`** (`src/lib/funnels.ts`). Use exactly these source values so each platform gets one bucket:

| YouTube channel link 1 | `…/free-guide?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=channel_links` |

Notes:
- Keep the sources to **lower-case, single words** (`tiktok`, `instagram`, `youtube`, `facebook`). `TikTok` and `tiktok` would end up in the same bucket anyway, but `tik_tok` would not.
- Linktree-style link-in-bio tools sit between the platform and the site and can hide the true referrer. If you use one, tag **every** link inside it. A link-hub page on principeai.com itself would keep attribution clean, but none exists in the repo today (gap; see open questions).
- **Correction to the brief:** `src/config/site.ts` social links *are* rendered once filled. `site-footer.tsx` renders them in the footer, and the home page adds them to the JSON-LD `sameAs`. Fill each URL as soon as the account exists.

## 3. TikTok

> **Context, Oct 2026.** The US app now runs under TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC (closed 2026-01-22). The JV is retraining the US recommendation model on US data. Reports of reach and RPM drops are anecdotal [R·S15]. Plan for TikTok to be **volatile**: use it as the hook-testing and discovery channel, not the only funnel.

### 3.1 Account type: Business, then apply for business verification

- **Why Business, for this brand.**
  - TikTok's own rule is that *any* content promoting a brand, product or service, including your own business, should use the Commercial Music Library (CML). "The licenses we hold for music outside of the CML don't cover the commercial use of music" [V·S4]. So the main thing a Personal account offers (the full music library) is something a business shouldn't use on promotional posts anyway.
  - Business accounts only see CML sounds [V·S5].
  - A **Verified Business Account (VBA)** unlocks a clickable bio link with no follower minimum [V·S1], native DM automations [V·S3], messaging lead collection, an AI chatbot and **"Organic Conversion Anchors (ability to attach destination link… to videos or profile)"** [V·S2]. That last one is the only way to put a clickable link *on* organic TikTok videos.
  - Business verification is available in the US [V·S2]. The required documents and review time were not on the page (UNVERIFIED). Expect to need Principe Consults' registration details.
- **The trade-off.** The Creator Rewards Program reportedly requires a Personal account, 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in 30 days and videos over 1 minute [R·S18]. It isn't relevant to a lead-generation goal.
- **Fallback if verification fails.** Stay on Business, use the profile email button and the Instagram or YouTube links in the social links section, and get to 1,000 followers for the bio link [V·S1].

### 3.3 Video length

- Upload up to 60 min, record in-app up to 10 min [R·S19] (UNVERIFIED officially).
- Socialinsider (Jan–Jun 2026, business accounts): engagement is highest **under 30 s** (6.0% of views) and views **peak at 120–180 s** [R·S14].
- **For Principe AI:** use 20–45 s for "prompt of the day" and quick tips (engagement and follows), and 60–120 s for screen-recorded demos (views, saves, authority). Don't go past 3 min at launch.

### 3.4 Captions, keywords, hashtags

- Caption: 4,000 characters in-app, but **2,200 via the API or schedulers** [R·S19]. Lead with the keyword phrase in the first ~80 characters [R].
- Use 3–5 specific hashtags (`#chatgpttips #aiforbusiness #smallbusinesstips #aitools #promptengineering`). Skip generic tags like `#fyp` [R].
- Before each weekly batch, open **Creator Search Insights**, filter by content gap, and script 1–2 videos per batch around those searches [V·S9].
- Turn on auto-captions or burn in your own. Spoken words are indexed [R].

### 3.5 Posting cadence and times (solo creator)

- Buffer (11.4M posts, Oct 2025): 2–5 posts a week gives up to +17% views per post over 1 a week. 6–10 a week gives up to +29%, but the gain comes from more chances at a breakout, not better posts [R·S13].
- **Plan: 4 a week at launch, 3 a week as the floor.**
- Buffer 2026 top slots in viewer-local time, by median engagement rate across 7.1M posts [R·S12]: Mon 1 pm · Tue 6 am · Wed 10 pm · Thu 1 pm · Fri 6 pm · Sat 5 pm · Sun 9 am.
- **Suggested ET slots:** Mon 1:00 pm, Wed 9:00 pm, Thu 1:00 pm, Sat 5:00 pm. Pacific viewers see these 3 hours earlier, so the midday slots work for both coasts. Adjust after 4 weeks using TikTok Studio's follower-activity view.

### 3.6 Formats that work for AI and tech educators on TikTok

1. **Prompt of the day:** green-screen the founder over a full-screen shot of the prompt, then a 5–10 s screen recording of the result. Source: the 50 prompts in `src/content/starter-kit.ts`, enough for 50 episodes.
2. **"Watch me do it in 60 seconds"** screen-recording demo with a face-cam bubble. Source: `deliverables/video-scripts/ai-for-business/07–15` (emails, SOPs, proposals, support) and the `ai-automation-accelerator/07–10` blueprints.
3. **Myth vs fact, green screen:** e.g. "Why ChatGPT makes things up" (from `ai-essentials/02-why-ai-makes-things-up.md`) or "What never to paste into AI" (from `16-safety-ethics…`). Commentary adds new information, so it counts as original everywhere.
4. **Reply-to-comment videos.** Answer a viewer's question on camera. This builds community and gives you endless prompts.
5. **The 7-Day AI Challenge** series (Day 1…Day 7 from the starter kit's 7-day plan), numbered so people follow to get the next day. Strong for New Year (section 7).
6. **Talking head + on-screen text listicles** ("3 places AI pays off in a small business"), from `ai-for-business/01-where-ai-pays-off.md`.

### 3.7 Trending audio vs original audio

- For promotional posts (anything that pushes a Principe AI offer), use **CML sounds or your own voice only** [V·S4]. If you use an original sound or non-CML music with the content-disclosure toggle on, you must accept the Music Usage Confirmation [V·S4].
- Educators win on voice. Use trends as **formats** (a meme structure, a "POV:" setup) rather than copyrighted songs. Keep any CML background track 15–20 dB under the voice.

### 3.8 Required toggles before posting

- **Content disclosure → "Your brand"** on any video that promotes Principe AI's courses, coaching, consulting or free offers. The post gets a "Promotional content" label, and TikTok says this "won't affect the distribution of your post" [V·S6].
- **AI-generated content** toggle only when section 2.5 says so [V·S7].

### 3.9 Cross-posting rules

- No watermarked uploads. Unoriginal or reused content with nothing new can't reach the For You feed [V·S8].
- Visible watermarks from other apps are widely reported to make videos ineligible [R·S20b]. TikTok's own For You feed standards page could not be fetched in this session (UNVERIFIED wording).
- Post TikTok first, or on the same day as the other platforms. Rewrite the on-screen title to the phrase TikTok users search (from Creator Search Insights).

### 3.10 Comment-to-DM and keyword automation

- **Native:** welcome message, keyword reply (exact match), suggested questions and chat prompts. These are **only for accounts with Advanced Access or a VBA** [V·S3]. Messages go through a 1–5 day review [R·S3b].
- **Comment-triggered DMs:** not generally available in the US as of Oct 2026. ManyChat's TikTok beta lists "Comment-to-message triggers" as "more to come", supports Business accounts only, and its launch post excluded EU, UK and US accounts [V·S10]. A vendor reports that US DM-API access opened around Nov 2025, with comment triggers only partially rolled out in Vietnam, Thailand and Indonesia [R·S11]. The two sources conflict, so treat US comment automation as **not available**.
- **The pattern that works today:** "DM me **KIT** and I'll send you the 50-prompt AI Starter Kit". A native keyword reply sends the UTM-tagged `/free-guide` link (needs VBA or Advanced Access). Until you have those, say "link in my profile".

### 3.11 Driving traffic off TikTok without suppression

- No clickable links in captions. A pasted URL in a caption is plain text (UNVERIFIED whether it is penalised; just don't do it).
- In order of strength: **VBA video destination link** [V·S2] > bio link (VBA or 1,000+ followers) [V·S1] > DM keyword reply [V·S3] > "search Principe AI on Instagram or YouTube".
- No official statement says "link in bio" CTAs are suppressed (UNVERIFIED either way). Keep the CTA spoken once, at the end, with the value up front.

### 4.3 Video length

- Up to 20 min, but "videos over 3 minutes will not be recommended to new audiences" [V·S22]. Boosting a Reel reportedly requires ≤ 90 s and 9:16 [R].
- Socialinsider (Jan–Jun 2026): views and engagement peak at **45–60 s**, with a second bump at 120–180 s. Everything past 180 s drops sharply [R·S14].
- **For Principe AI:** use 30–60 s as the default and 90–120 s for demos you might boost later. Never exceed 3:00.

### 4.4 Captions, keywords, hashtags

- First caption line is the keyword-led title (it also becomes the Google snippet): `ChatGPT prompt for follow-up emails that actually get replies` (61 chars).
- Then 2–4 lines of value (the steps), the CTA, and **at most 5 hashtags**. The cap is 5 [R·S27][R·S28]. Keep hashtags in the caption, not the comments [V·S24].
- Add alt text or captions (auto-captions on). Keep text inside the 4:5 safe zone, because the grid now crops Reels to a rectangle [R].

### 4.5 Cadence and times

- Buffer (2.1M posts, Aug 2025): **3–5 posts a week** roughly doubles follower growth rate over 1–2 a week and gives ~12% more reach per post [R·S34].
- Buffer 2026 (9.6M posts, viewer-local time): top slots Thu 9 am, Wed 12 pm and Wed 6 pm. **Reels do best in the evening, 6–11 pm**, with Wed and Thu strongest [R·S33].
- **Suggested ET slots:** Mon 7:00 pm · Wed 12:00 pm · Thu 9:00 am · Sat 9:00 pm.
- Add **Stories 3–5 days a week**: behind the scenes, a poll, and a link sticker to the free kit. Story link stickers are open to everyone [V·S35].

### 4.6 Best formats on Instagram

Same core formats as TikTok (3.6), with tweaks:
- **Carousel companion.** For every 3rd Reel, post a carousel of the prompt or steps. It is saveable and sendable, and since Apr 2026 photos and carousels also fall under the originality rules [V·S25], so make your own designs.
- **"Send this to…" endings** to drive sends, Instagram's strongest signal for reaching non-followers [R·S28].
- **Collab posts** with a complementary small-business creator (accountant, realtor coach). Both audiences see the post and it stays original [V·S25].

### 4.7 Audio

- The audio page is one of Instagram's top-four ranking predictions [V·S23]. Trending audio can help, but for teaching content your **voice is the content**.
- Use licensed or royalty-free tracks quietly under the voiceover. Name the original audio "Principe AI · [topic]" so reuse credits you.

### 4.8 Comment-to-DM automation

- **Native, free:** Meta Business Suite Inbox → Automations → template "Comment to message: Reply to a comment that contains specific hashtags." **Only on Meta Business Suite desktop** [V·S32]. The trigger is a hashtag, so the CTA becomes "Comment **#prompts** and I'll DM you the kit." Vendors report up to 4 hashtag and response pairs [R].
- **Third-party (more flexible: any keyword, public reply, follow-up questions):** ManyChat and similar tools on Meta's API. 2026 pricing conflicts: one source says the free tier dropped to 25 contacts in Mar 2026, another says 1,000 contacts; Pro is $15–29/mo [R·S36] (UNVERIFIED; check before buying).
- Meta API limits as reported by vendors: **one private reply per comment, within 7 days**, and the thread only continues if the person replies [R·S36b] (UNVERIFIED on Meta's developer docs).
- **Policy caution:** keep the comment ask a delivery mechanism ("comment #prompts to get the kit"), not vote-baiting ("comment YES if you agree"). Meta tells monetised creators to avoid engagement bait [R·S55]. Meta has not said whether keyword-for-DM prompts are demoted (UNVERIFIED), and Meta itself is testing keyword-reply ads [R].

### 4.9 Cross-posting rules

- Make your own footage. "Watermarked reels… or reels that have already been posted on Instagram" are made less visible [V·S23]. Never re-upload a TikTok download.
- Check **Settings → Account Status** monthly. It shows whether the account can be recommended to non-followers [V·S25].

### 4.10 Driving traffic off Instagram

- In order of strength: **comment-to-DM link** (most direct, and DMs are a positive signal) > **Story link sticker** [V·S35] > **5 bio links** [V·S21] > "link in bio" CTA.
- **Clickable links in Reels captions** are only on paid Meta One Advanced ($49.99/mo: 4 IG posts and 4 IG Reels a month), Expert (8 and 8) and Max (12 and 12) [V·S29][R·S28]. That isn't worth it at launch. Re-evaluate once a single Reel reliably drives leads.

## 8. Recommendations for Principe AI

### 8.1 Platform priority

| Tier | Platform | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1, must-do** | **Instagram Reels** | Best conversion path from zero followers: 5 bio links, Story link stickers, free native comment-to-DM, Trial Reels from 200 followers, Google-indexable captions. Reaches both the "for your life" and "for your business" audiences |
| **Tier 1, must-do** | **YouTube Shorts** | Search demand for "how to use ChatGPT / Claude for X" compounds. The Related-video link bridges to long-form (with clickable links), and long-form drafts already exist (56 lesson scripts plus 3 welcome videos). The channel also hosts the unlisted lesson videos |
| **Tier 1, must-do (discovery)** | **TikTok** | Still the strongest reach to non-followers for a new account and the best place to test hooks cheaply. But it is volatile after the 2026 JV, links are gated behind VBA or 1,000 followers, and there's no US comment-to-DM. Go Business plus VBA on day 1 |
| **Tier 2** | **Facebook Reels** | Near-zero marginal effort through auto cross-posting from Instagram, plus good reach for longer demos among older business owners. Promote it to Tier 1 if Admin → Funnels shows `facebook` within 25% of the top source after 60 days |
| **Tier 3, later (months 3+)** | Weekly YouTube long-form tutorials, IG and TikTok LIVE Q&As, Meta One paid links, TikTok Creator Search ads | Only after the short-form engine runs for 8 weeks and one lead source is proven |

### 8.2 Realistic weekly cadence and time cost (solo founder)

**Standard plan: 4 short videos a week**, each posted natively to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, auto-cross-posted to Facebook, plus 3–5 Instagram Stories.

| Task | Time / week |
|---|---|
| Plan and script 4 videos from existing assets (starter kit, lesson scripts, Creator Search Insights) | 45 min |
| Record in one batch session (face-cam takes plus screen recordings) | 1 h 30 min |
| Edit 4 masters (jump cuts, zooms, on-screen text, captions) at ~25 min each | 1 h 40 min |
| Upload, caption, toggles and schedule on 3 platforms (~8 min × 4 × 3) | 1 h 35 min |
| Stories (3–5 × 5 min) | 20 min |
| Community: reply to comments and DMs, 15 min × 6 days | 1 h 30 min |
| Weekly review: Admin → Funnels by source, plus each platform's retention metric | 30 min |
| **Total** | **≈ 7 h 50 min a week** |

**Lean plan when busy (floor): 3 videos a week, ≈ 5 h 30 min.** It still sits inside Buffer's "2–5 a week" TikTok band and "3–5 a week" Instagram band [S13][S34].

**Kill-or-keep rule:** after 30 videos (about 8 weeks), cut or downgrade any platform that delivers < 10% of social-sourced leads *and* < 10% of social-sourced visitors in Admin → Funnels.

### 8.3 The 5 most important do's

1. **Do film a real human (the founder) using real AI tools.** Export one clean master with no platform watermark, post it natively everywhere, and change only the caption, keywords and CTA. That is "original" under every platform's 2026 rules [V·S25][V·S50][V·S8].
2. **Do put the search phrase in four places:** the name field (`Principe AI | AI Tips & Tools`), spoken in the first 3 seconds, the on-screen title, and the first caption line [V·S24][V·S9].
3. **Do give each video one free offer and deliver it the way each platform allows:** IG and FB comment `#prompts` → auto-DM [V·S32], TikTok "DM me KIT" → keyword reply [V·S3] or the bio link, YouTube Related video → long-form → description link [V·S40]. Use a UTM link with lower-case `utm_source` per platform every time (2.7).
4. **Do unlock links on day 1:** TikTok Business plus verification [V·S1][V·S2], YouTube advanced features [V·S40], all 5 Instagram bio links [V·S21]. Fill the social URLs in `src/config/site.ts` so the footer and JSON-LD link the profiles.
5. **Do set the disclosure toggles every time:** TikTok "Your brand" on promotional posts (no reach penalty [V·S6]), and AI labels only for realistic synthetic media (2.5). Check Instagram Account Status monthly [V·S25].

### 8.4 The 5 most important don'ts

1. **Don't re-upload watermarked or downloaded files**, and don't post the same file twice on one platform [V·S23][R·S48].
2. **Don't use trending commercial songs on promotional posts.** TikTok's music licences don't cover commercial use outside the CML [V·S4], and YouTube blocks Shorts over 1 min that have any Content ID claim [V·S41].
3. **Don't paste URLs where they aren't clickable** (Shorts descriptions and comments, TikTok captions), and **don't use more than 5 hashtags on Instagram** or put keywords in the comments [V·S40][R·S27][V·S24].
4. **Don't fabricate.** No invented results, testimonials, income claims, "students" or fake scarcity. That fits the brand voice and TikTok's fake-engagement and fake-review rules [V·S8]. Don't use an AI avatar or cloned voice without the labels in 2.5.
5. **Don't bait or overpay early.** No "comment YES", no "follow or you'll miss part 2" [V·S8], and no paid Meta One links ($49.99+/mo) until a free path (DM, Story link, bio) is proven to convert [V·S29].
