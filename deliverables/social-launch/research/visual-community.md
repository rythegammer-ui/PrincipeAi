# Visual and community playbook, October 2026: Instagram (carousels, Stories, Highlights, Broadcast Channels), Pinterest, Facebook Page and Groups, Meta Business Suite

Research for the Principe AI social launch, compiled 2026-10-20. It is written for a solo founder starting from zero followers, based in the US (America/New_York), who sells practical AI education through free lead magnets: the AI Starter Kit, the AI Readiness Assessment and free calls. Paid offers are $47–$497 courses, $150 coaching and quoted done-with-you implementation.

Reels and short video are covered in the sibling file `short-video.md`. This file covers the **static, Stories and community** side of Instagram and Facebook, plus Pinterest and Meta Business Suite.

**How to read the evidence tags**

- **[V]** Verified on the platform's own help centre, newsroom, creator blog or developer docs, accessed 2026-10-20. The source number is in brackets, e.g. [V·S3].
- **[R]** Reported by a reputable third party (dated). Treat it as likely, but check it in the app.
- **UNVERIFIED**: I could not confirm it from a primary source in this session. Check it in the app before relying on it.
- **Practitioner guidance**: my recommendation, not a platform rule.

## 2. Instagram: feed carousels, Stories, Highlights, Broadcast Channels

### 2.1 Account and profile setup (one-off, about 2 hours)

1. **Professional account, Business type.** Instagram has two professional types: Business suits brands and service providers, and Creator suits public figures [V·S37a]. Choose **Business**, because it carries action buttons and connects cleanly to a Facebook Page, Meta Business Suite and later ads [V·S2]. That keeps it consistent with `short-video.md`.
   - Business accounts reportedly have a smaller licensed-music library (UNVERIFIED). Use original voice, Meta's royalty-free audio or no music.
2. **Username.** `@principeai` is the first choice. Fall backs are `@principe.ai` and `@principeaihq`. Availability is UNVERIFIED. Use the **same handle on every platform** so UTM tags and mentions stay consistent.
3. **Name field.** This bold line is searchable in Instagram search, unlike most of the bio. Third-party guides agree on this, though Instagram's own docs do not say so [R·S15b]. Suggested: **`Principe AI | AI for Business & Life`** (36 characters; the character limit itself is UNVERIFIED).
4. **Bio (150 characters max)** [R, consistent across guides]. Two drafts, both checked for length:
   - A (both paths, 128 characters):
     ```
     Practical AI for your life and business.
     Assess → Learn → Implement.
     Free AI Starter Kit: 50 copy-paste prompts + a 7-day plan ↓
     ```
   - B (business-led, 133 characters):
     ```
     AI you will actually use: courses, coaching and done-with-you AI systems for business owners.
     Start free: 50 prompts + a 7-day plan ↓
     ```
   - No "#1", no student counts and no "as seen in". There are no testimonials yet.
5. **Links: up to 5** under Edit profile → Links. Only the first one shows on the profile; the others appear when someone taps it. Links can be reordered [V·S1]. Meta One Essential raises this to a 20-link "link page" [R·S30], which is not needed now. Order:
   1. `https://principeai.com/free-guide?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=starter-kit&utm_content=bio-link-1`
   2. `https://principeai.com/assessment?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=assessment&utm_content=bio-link-2`
   3. `https://principeai.com/courses?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=courses&utm_content=bio-link-3`
   4. `https://principeai.com/consulting?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=implementation&utm_content=bio-link-4`
   5. `https://principeai.com/book/ai-strategy-call?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=strategy-call&utm_content=bio-link-5`
   - During a promotion, move that page to position 1. For example, put the course sale at the top during Black Friday week.
6. **Action button.** Business accounts can add one under Edit profile → Action buttons [V·S2]. The options are mostly partner integrations, and whether a custom booking URL is allowed is UNVERIFIED. If no partner option fits, skip it: the 5th profile link already goes to booking.
7. **Profile photo.** The profile photo, plus the founder's face in Stories and Reels, carries trust for a one-person education brand. Mosseri's memo argues credibility comes from "who posted it" [R·S22].
   - Option one: the brand mark on royal violet (#6938ef) with a gold (#fdb022) accent, readable at about 110 px.
   - Option two, if the founder is comfortable on camera: a face photo on a violet background.
   - Practitioner judgment. Either works. Just do not switch often.
8. **Connect the Facebook Page** in Accounts Center (needed for Meta Business Suite and cross-posting) [V·S37].
9. **Search engine visibility.** Public professional accounts held by someone 18+ can have posts, Reels and **Story Highlights indexed by Google** [V·S24]. Write captions and Highlight titles in plain keyword language, e.g. "ChatGPT prompts for small business owners".
10. **AI labelling.** Photorealistic AI video or realistic AI audio **must** be disclosed with Meta's AI label [V·S28]. AI-assisted captions, edits and graphics do not need the "AI-generated profile" label [R·S27]. Never run Principe AI as an AI persona or avatar account; that is exactly what Instagram now down-ranks [R·S27].
11. **Add the profile URLs to the site.** In `src/config/site.ts`, `social.instagram`, `social.facebook` and `social.pinterest` are empty. The comment says filled ones appear in the footer and the structured data. Fill them on launch day.

### 2.2 Feed carousels in 2026

**Hard facts**

| Item | Value | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Max items per carousel | 20 photos and/or videos | [V·S8][R·S9] |
| Supported photo ratios | 1:1, 4:5, and 3:4 natively since 2025 | [R·S10][R·S13] |
| Ratio rule | The first slide sets the ratio for all slides | [R·S13] |
| Grid display | Rectangular, taller-than-square thumbnails; posts open at full size | [R·S11] |
| Caption length | 2,200 characters | [V·S16] (developer docs) |
| Hashtags | 5 max (caption and comments counted together, per guides) | [R·S14][R·S15] |
| Scheduling | Up to 30 days ahead for free | [V·S34] |

**4:5 or 3:4? Use 4:5 (1080×1350) as the master.**

- 3:4 (1080×1440) fills slightly more of the phone screen and matches the grid thumbnail.
- One 2026 guide warns that Instagram's publishing API still documents a "4:5 to 1.91:1" range, so schedulers may crop a 3:4 file to 4:5 [R·S13]. That is a single source, so treat it as UNVERIFIED.
- 4:5 also cross-posts to Facebook and LinkedIn without surprises.
- **Rule:** design at 1080×1350. Keep all text inside a centred safe area of roughly 1010×1260 so the 3:4 grid crop never cuts a word.
- If you post from the Instagram app and want the extra height, 1080×1440 works [R·S13]. Never mix ratios inside one carousel.

**Why carousels matter for this brand.**

- Across large 2026 datasets, carousels beat Reels on engagement rate while Reels win on reach.
  - Buffer, 45M+ posts: median carousel engagement rate 6.90% vs 3.31% for Reels; Reels get 1.36× the reach [R·S64].
  - Socialinsider: 0.55% vs 0.52% [R·S64].
  - The gap depends on how each vendor defines engagement. The direction is consistent.
- Mosseri's top ranking signals are watch time, likes per reach and **sends per reach**, with sends weighing most for non-followers [R·S23, secondary].
- **Teaching carousels that people save and send** fit Principe AI's strengths: prompts, checklists and step-by-step workflows.
- Instagram says adding **audio to photos and carousels** can get them into the Reels tab [V·S17]. Add quiet, royalty-free audio to every carousel.

**Slide count and text density** (practitioner guidance; there is no platform rule)

- **6–10 slides** for a teaching post. 20 is a ceiling, not a target. Every slide has to earn the swipe.
- **Slide 1 (hook):** one promise in at most 8 words. Example: "*5 prompts* that clear your inbox." Use Instrument Serif italic on one word, as the site does.
- **Slide 2 (second hook):** restate the payoff or show the before/after. Some guides report that Instagram sometimes shows a carousel again starting from a later slide (UNVERIFIED).
- **Body slides:** one idea per slide, **at most about 30 words**, body text at least 40 px at 1080 width, headline 72–96 px, outer margins about 90 px, a slide counter ("3/8") and the small "principeai.com" wordmark in a corner.
- **Prompt slides:** show the full copy-paste prompt in a mono or code-style box on an ink (#0c0a1d) band with light text. This mirrors the site's prose code style and makes slides worth saving.
- **Last slide (CTA):** one action only. Examples:
  - "Comment **KIT** and I'll DM you the free Starter Kit." This needs a comment-to-DM automation; see `short-video.md` for the native Meta tool and third-party options.
  - "Get all 50 prompts free: link in bio."
- **Caption:** first line repeats the hook with a keyword ("ChatGPT prompts for busy business owners"), then 3–6 short lines of value, the CTA, and **3–5 specific hashtags** (#aiforbusiness, #chatgptprompts, #smallbusinesstips, #aitools, #productivitytips). Hashtags are a topic signal, not a reach lever [R·S14].
- **Alt text:** add it to every slide (advanced settings). It helps accessibility and gives the platform an extra topic signal (the ranking benefit is UNVERIFIED).

### 2.3 Stories

**Features** [V·S6 unless noted]

- **Link sticker:** sends viewers to any URL. Open to all accounts **except new accounts and accounts that repeatedly break the Community Guidelines** [V·S5]. "New" is not defined. If the sticker is missing at launch, post daily for a week or two and check again (UNVERIFIED threshold).
  - A Story with a link sticker **cannot be boosted** [V·S7].
- **Poll:** 2 options with live results. **Quiz:** 4 options, one correct answer. **Questions:** private replies you can reshare as answers. **Emoji slider**, **Add Yours** and **Cutouts** are also in the tray [V·S6].
- **Countdown** sticker: listed in 2026 third-party guides [R].
- Stories disappear after 24 h unless saved to a Highlight [V·S26].
- **Scheduling:** Instagram's in-app "Schedule your story" (up to 30 days ahead) is **Meta One Advanced/Expert/Max only** [V·S35]. Whether Meta Business Suite still schedules Instagram Stories for free in 2026 is UNVERIFIED. It did historically [V·S38, older page]. Check the composer. If it's gone, post Stories live; they take 5–10 minutes.

**Story sequences for Principe AI** (practitioner guidance)

- **"Poll → teach → link" (3–5 frames, 2–3× a week):**
  1. Poll: "Have you used ChatGPT this week? Yes / Not yet".
  2. One tip frame.
  3. One example-prompt frame.
  4. Link sticker to `/free-guide?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=starter-kit&utm_content=story-YYYYMMDD`.
- **"Ask me anything about AI" (weekly Questions box):** answer the best questions on later frames and turn the strongest into next week's Reel or carousel. This also gives you real questions in your audience's own words for Pinterest keywords and Facebook Group posts.
- **Quiz teaser for the Assessment:** "Which of these is safe to paste into a public AI tool?" (4 options), then the answer, then a link sticker to `/assessment?…&utm_content=story-quiz`.
- **Launch and Black Friday:** a Countdown sticker 3–5 days before, then a link sticker on the day. Use the discount the owner sets in Admin → Courses; never invent one.
- **Repost every new carousel and Reel to Stories** with a "new post" sticker. It's the cheapest distribution there is.
- **Cadence:** 3–7 frames on 4–6 days a week. Consistency matters more than volume.
- **Specs:** 9:16, 1080×1920. Keep the top and bottom ~250 px clear of text (standard practice, not re-verified this session).

### 2.7 Cadence and posting times

- **Feed:** 2 carousels a week, plus the Reels from `short-video.md`. Post the carousel on its own day, not the same day as a Reel.
- **Stories:** 4–6 days a week.
- **Engagement:** 15 min after each post replying to every comment (comments are early signals), plus 10 min a day of genuine comments on 5–10 accounts in your niche (small-business owners, coaches, local businesses, AI educators).
- **Posting times** (America/New_York; vendor data is based on each tool's own customers and doesn't fit every account):
  - Sprout Social 2026: Mon 2–4 pm, Tue 1–7 pm, Wed 12–9 pm, Thu 12–2 pm local [R·S63].
  - Buffer, 9.6M posts (updated Sep 2026): Thu 9 am, Wed 12 pm, Wed 6 pm. Fri and Sat are weakest [R·S63].
  - **Start with Tue/Wed/Thu 12:00–1:30 pm ET.** That is 9–10:30 am Pacific, so it catches both coasts. Test one 6–7 pm ET slot. After 4 weeks, use Instagram Insights' "most active times".
- **Collab posts** with complementary accounts (bookkeepers, marketing coaches, local chambers) are a good zero-follower growth lever. Mechanics UNVERIFIED this session.

## 3. Pinterest

### 3.1 Why it fits, and the honest caveats

- **Fit:** Pinterest is search-led and long-lived. Pinterest says "there's no set engagement window… a Pin can gain engagement hours, days, months, or even years after it's first published" [V·S54]. That suits a solo founder: Pins made now keep working.
- **Scale:** 640M global monthly active users in Q2 2026, +11% year on year [V·S61]. Gen Z was "over half" of users per the Q2 2025 release [V·S61, 2025 figure]. No current US-only figure was found.
- **Audience match:** the **"For your life"** path fits Pinterest especially well (meal plans, trip itineraries, budgets, learning plans). These are the categories already in the Starter Kit. Small-business tips also do well (UNVERIFIED for this niche; validate in Pinterest Trends).
- **Caveat:** some publishers reported steep outbound-click declines after 2025 algorithm changes [R·S62, single affected-publisher source]. Treat Pinterest as **Tier 2**: worth a cheap, consistent effort, not the main bet.
- **Caveat:** Pinterest labels AI-modified images and lets users see fewer of them [V·S59]. Use real screenshots, typography and brand graphics, not AI-generated scenes.

### 3.2 Account setup (one-off, about 3 hours)

1. **Business account** (free). Create a new one or convert a personal one; it must be public [V·S49].
   - Display name, keyword-led: **`Principe AI | AI Prompts & Tools for Small Business`** (51 characters; the limit is UNVERIFIED).
   - About text: one sentence on who it's for, plus the Starter Kit offer.
2. **Claim principeai.com** under Settings → Claimed accounts / Link to Pinterest → Websites → Claim [V·S50]. You must own the domain and be able to edit the site; a site can be claimed by only one Pinterest account [V·S50]. Methods:
   - **DNS TXT record**: no code needed, may take up to 72 h [V·S50]. Recommended.
   - **HTML tag in `<head>`**: in this Next.js app it would go in the root `metadata` in `src/app/layout.tsx` as `verification: { other: { "p:domain_verify": "<code>" } }`. Next's `verification.other` emits `<meta name="…" content="…">` [V·S66, local Next docs]. That is a code change for the site owner, not part of this research.
   - HTML file at the server root, or Google Merchant Center [V·S50].
   - A claimed site attributes Pins from your domain to you and unlocks website analytics (UNVERIFIED detail).
3. **Rich Pins** are still documented, in article, product and recipe types, and use Open Graph or Schema.org metadata from the page [V·S51].
   - Product rich Pins show live price and availability only if merchant guidelines are met [V·S51].
   - The site's root metadata uses `og:type: "website"`. Whether course pages qualify for article or product rich Pins, and whether Pinterest still auto-applies them after claiming, is **UNVERIFIED**. This is an open item for the developer, not a launch blocker.
4. **Link Instagram (optional).** This auto-publishes Instagram posts as Pins. It needs an Instagram professional, public account, creates a "Social" board, can import the last 90/180/365 days, and new Pins can take up to 24 h to appear [V·S57].
   - Turn it **off** at launch. Native 2:3 Pins that link to your site do the job better, and auto-published Pins may link back to Instagram rather than your site (UNVERIFIED).
5. **Pinterest tag** (conversion pixel): Tier 3. It needs a privacy-policy and cookie review, and it only matters if you run Pinterest ads.

### 3.3 Pin formats and specs (2026)

| Format | Spec | Tag |
|---|---|---|

### 5.2 Joining other people's groups: etiquette and self-promotion norms (Tier 2, start week 2)

**Mechanics**

- A Page can only **request** to join a group; groups can't invite Pages, and admins may not allow Pages [V·S47].
- In others' groups, participate from the **founder's personal profile** ("[YOUR NAME], founder of Principe AI" in the profile intro and work field). Peer-to-peer advice reads as more trustworthy than a brand Page (practitioner guidance).

**Norms seen across groups** [R·S48]

- Read the pinned rules and membership questions, and ask moderators before posting anything promotional.
- Common regimes are: a total ban; a weekly promo day or thread; a per-member monthly cap; and **reply-only** promotion (you may name your business only when someone asks for a recommendation).
- "Give before you take" groups shut down drive-by promotion fast. Disguised promotions, such as giveaways hiding an offer, get reported and removed.

**Principe AI routine** (practitioner guidance)

- Join **5–8 active groups** where the buyers are: small-business owner groups, solopreneur and freelancer groups, local business or chamber groups, industry groups (real estate, coaching, bookkeeping, trades), and AI-for-beginners groups. Pick groups with daily posts and active moderators. Avoid link-dump groups. No specific groups were vetted in this session.
- **20 minutes, 4 days a week.** Answer 3–5 real questions fully. Include a copy-paste prompt or a 3-step workflow in the answer itself. Link nothing unless the group allows it or the person asks.
- Use each group's **official promo thread** weekly. Post the Starter Kit link with `utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=starter-kit&utm_content=group-<shortname>` so Admin → Funnels shows which groups convert.
- **Never post the same link or promo across several groups in a row.** That looks like spam to members and moderators, and to Meta's spam systems. Meta's exact spam thresholds were not verified this session (UNVERIFIED). It is still the right default.
- Keep a simple log of each group's rules, promo day and what has worked.

## 7. UTM conventions for these platforms (they matter for Admin → Funnels)

The site records first-touch UTM parameters in a cookie. `src/lib/funnels.ts` attributes **channel = `utm_source`**, and the demo seed uses `utm_medium=social` for organic social. So:

- **`utm_source`**, always lowercase and always the same spelling: `instagram`, `pinterest`, `facebook`. Never `ig`, `IG`, `fb` or `Instagram`; variants split the Funnels rows.
- **`utm_medium`**: `social` for all organic posts, profiles and groups. Reserve `paid-social` for ads later.
- **`utm_campaign`**: the offer, e.g. `starter-kit`, `assessment`, `strategy-call`, `implementation`, `courses`, `launch-2026`, `bfcm-2026`, `new-year-2027`.
- **`utm_content`**: the placement, e.g. `bio-link-1`, `story-YYYYMMDD`, `story-quiz`, `page-cta`, `fb-linkpost-launch`, `first-comment`, `group-<shortname>`, `pin-<design>-v1`, `dm-kit`.
- Build links in a spreadsheet so each one is typed once. Never retype a UTM by hand on a phone.
