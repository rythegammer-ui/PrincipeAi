## Script

### 1. Welcome · 0:00

**[ON CAMERA]**

Hi, I'm [YOUR NAME], and welcome to AI Automation Accelerator: Build Workflows & Agents. AI can now read, sort, draft and decide, but only when it's connected to your tools with the right checks around it. This course is about building automations that run reliably in production, not demos that break in week two.

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "AI Automation Accelerator: Build Workflows & Agents"]**

### 2. Who it's for · 0:25

**[ON CAMERA]**

This course is for builders, operators and ambitious owners who already use AI assistants and want to put them to work inside their tools. You don't need any previous experience with automation platforms. If you're brand new to AI, start with AI Essentials, then come back.

### 3. What you'll be able to do · 0:45

**[ON CAMERA]**

By the end, you'll be able to design AI workflows with a clear trigger, context, model step, action and checks. You'll connect AI to your apps with Zapier, Make or n8n, and get structured output you can trust. You'll handle errors, retries and human review so nothing slips through the cracks.

And you'll design simple AI agents with well-scoped tools, guardrails and approvals, then test, monitor and secure what you build.

### 4. How it's organized · 1:15

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Automation Foundations · No-Code AI Workflows · Real Automations to Build · AI Agents · Ship It"]**

The course has five modules. Automation Foundations and No-Code AI Workflows give you the patterns. In Real Automations to Build, you'll work through four blueprints: lead qualification, a content pipeline, inbox triage and a weekly reporting digest.

Then AI Agents covers when an agent is the right choice and how to keep it safe. And Ship It covers testing, monitoring, costs, security and a 30-day plan.

If you follow that plan, you'll finish with at least one automation running in production with tests, monitoring and a runbook, blueprints for three more, and a designed agent ready to pilot.

### 5. How to get the most from each lesson · 1:55

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Watch · Read · Build it"]**

For each lesson, watch the video, then read the written lesson, where you'll find the copy-paste prompts, templates and checklists. Then do the "Build it" exercise. If you're taking the course on the Principe AI website, every lesson also has an AI tutor you can ask questions.

### 6. What you need · 2:15

**[ON CAMERA]**

You'll need an account on one automation platform, and most offer a free tier or trial to learn on. You'll need access to an AI model, through your platform's AI step or an API key from a provider. And you'll need the apps you already use, like email, a spreadsheet and a CRM.

No coding is required, and the optional code examples are there for the curious. Tool subscriptions and AI usage are paid separately from the course.

### 7. Let's begin · 2:50

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Next: Lesson 1 · Anatomy of an AI Workflow"]**

Head to Lesson 1, Anatomy of an AI Workflow, where you'll learn the five parts behind every dependable AI automation. See you there.
