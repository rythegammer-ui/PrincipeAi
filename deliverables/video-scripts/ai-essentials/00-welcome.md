## Before you record

- Replace [YOUR NAME] in the opening line with your name.
- No screen recording is needed. Find a quiet spot with good light and look straight at the camera.
- Ask your editor to prepare the five module names as a simple title card.

## Script

### 1. Welcome · 0:00

**[ON CAMERA]**

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "AI Essentials: Use AI in Everyday Life"]**

Hi, I'm [YOUR NAME], and welcome to AI Essentials: Use AI in Everyday Life. AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini can draft your emails, plan your week and explain almost anything at your level. But only if you know how to talk to them, and when not to trust them.

This course is for complete beginners. If you can send an email or a text message, you can take this course. Everything is explained in plain English, step by step.

### 2. What you'll be able to do · 0:35

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "What you'll learn"]**

By the end, you'll understand what AI is, how it works and why it sometimes makes things up. You'll write prompts that get great answers the first time, and you'll know how to fix the ones that don't.

You'll use AI to write messages, plan, learn, research and make decisions. And you'll build the safety habits that matter: protecting your privacy, fact-checking answers and spotting AI-powered scams.

### 3. How the course works · 1:05

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Getting Started · Prompting That Works · AI for Everyday Life · AI for Work & Productivity · Make It a Habit"]**

There are 18 short lessons in five modules. We begin with Getting Started, then move to Prompting That Works, where you'll learn the simple five-part prompt behind great answers. Then you'll put it to use in AI for Everyday Life and AI for Work & Productivity.

We finish with Make It a Habit, where you'll build your personal AI toolkit and a daily habit that lasts.

### 4. Get the most from each lesson · 1:30

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Watch · Read · Try it now"]**

Each lesson works best in three steps. Watch the video, then read the written lesson, which has the full copy-paste prompts and templates. Then do the "Try it now" exercise with your AI assistant open, because you learn this by doing.

If you're taking the course on the Principe AI website, every lesson also has an AI tutor you can ask questions.

### 5. What you need · 2:00

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Claude · ChatGPT · Gemini · Microsoft Copilot"]**

All you need is one of the major AI assistants, such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Microsoft Copilot. The free version is enough for every lesson, and if you don't have one yet, Lesson 3 walks you through choosing one and setting it up safely.

Tools and features change quickly, so this course focuses on the skills that carry across all of them. Giving good context, refining answers, verifying facts and protecting your privacy work with any assistant, today and in the future.

### 6. Let's begin · 2:35

**[ON CAMERA]**

So, are you ready? Head to Lesson 1, What AI Really Is (and Isn't), and let's take you from curious to confident. I'll see you there.
