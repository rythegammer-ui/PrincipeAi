# Lesson 18: Your 30-Day AI Habit Plan

| | |
|---|---|

**Goal of this video:** Start a simple four-week plan, about ten minutes a day, that turns everything from the course into a lasting AI habit.

## Before you record

- Open your AI assistant in a new chat.
- Prepare a short sample log to paste in place of the bracket: a handful of made-up rows with a date, a task, the time saved and one lesson learned. You say on camera that it's a sample.
- Optional B-roll: a notes app with a four-column log, or a calendar reminder for Day 30.

## Script

### 1. Hook · 0:00

**[ON CAMERA]**

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Your 30-Day AI Habit Plan"]**

Knowing how to use AI and actually using it are two different things. The people who get the most from AI aren't necessarily the most technical. They're the ones who made it a habit.

This final lesson gives you a 30-day plan to do exactly that, in about ten minutes a day.

### 2. How to make it stick · 0:20

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Attach it · Start small · Make progress visible"]**

Three principles make this plan work. First, attach it to something you already do, like "After my morning coffee, I'll use AI for one task." An existing routine is the best trigger for a new one.

Second, start small: ten minutes and one task, because small wins build momentum. Third, make progress visible with a simple log of the date, the task, a rough guess at the time saved, and one thing you learned.

### 3. The four weeks · 0:55

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Week 1: daily wins · Week 2: your system · Week 3: go deeper · Week 4: refine"]**

Week one is about daily wins: use AI once a day for something real. Draft a message you've been putting off, plan tomorrow from a brain dump, summarize a long article, and on day seven, review your log.

Week two builds your system. Refine your personal instructions, save your five most useful prompts as templates, set up a workspace, try voice or images, and run the three-minute fact-check.

In week three, you go deeper. Pick one meaningful project, like planning a trip or learning a new skill, and spend ten minutes a day moving it forward. Each day, try a technique you haven't used yet, like the Socratic tutor, a pre-mortem or a weighted comparison.

Week four is for refining. Improve your three most-used templates, try AI in a new area of your life, teach someone else one thing you've learned, and review your privacy settings and never-share rules.

### 4. Demo: your Day 30 reflection · 2:00

Then on day 30, you reflect. Here's the prompt, and I'm using a short sample log.

**[SCREEN RECORDING]** Paste this prompt:

~~~
Here's my log from 30 days of using AI: [PASTE YOUR LOG]
Help me reflect: which uses saved me the most time or stress, which
didn't help, and what patterns do you notice? Then suggest 3 new ways
to use AI next month, based on what has worked for me, each with a
prompt template.
~~~

It sorts out what helped and what didn't, spots the patterns, and suggests three new ways to use AI, each with a template. That's your plan for next month.

### 5. When you miss a day · 2:30

**[ON CAMERA]**

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "A missed day is a pause, not a failure"]**

You will miss a day, and that's fine. Don't restart the plan; just pick up where you left off. Missing a day doesn't break a habit, but quitting does.

If ten minutes feels like too much on a busy day, do a two-minute version: one message, or one quick question. And don't wait for the perfect use case, because small everyday tasks build the habit.

AI tools change fast, but you don't need to chase every announcement. Giving context, iterating, verifying and protecting your privacy work with any assistant, today and in the years ahead. When a new feature appears, ask your assistant to explain it, then try it on one real task.

### 6. Try it now · 3:20

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Try it now: do Day 1 today"]**

Choose your trigger, like "After my morning coffee, I'll use AI for one task." Then create your log with four columns: date, task, time saved and lesson learned.

Put a reminder in your calendar for Day 30. And then do Day 1 today.

### 7. Recap and congratulations · 3:40

To recap: tie AI to an existing routine, start with ten minutes a day, and log your wins. Missed days don't matter much, but consistency over time does.

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "You did it"]**

Congratulations on finishing AI Essentials! Think about how far you've come.

You started by learning what AI really is and why it sometimes makes things up. Then you learned the five-part prompt, how to iterate, and how to give AI your context. You've used it for messages, planning, learning, serious questions, writing, research and decisions.

And you've built the habits that keep you safe: fact-checking, protecting your privacy and spotting scams. You now have the skills, the toolkit and a plan to make AI genuinely useful in your everyday life. So keep your toolkit close.
