## Script

### 1. Welcome · 0:00

**[ON CAMERA]**

Hi, I'm [YOUR NAME], and welcome to AI for Business Owners: The Implementation Playbook. Most businesses have tried AI by now. Far fewer have changed how work actually gets done.

That gap isn't about the tools. It's about knowing where AI pays off, how to brief it properly, and how to roll it out so your team uses it safely and consistently.

### 2. Who this course is for · 0:25

This course is for owners, operators and managers of small and mid-size businesses. If you've tried an AI assistant and wondered what it should actually be doing in your business every week, you're in the right place.

You don't need to write any code. Everything works with any major AI assistant, whether that's Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Microsoft Copilot, alongside the email, CRM and no-code automation tools you already use.

### 3. What you'll be able to do · 0:55

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "AI drafts, people decide."]**

By the end, you'll be able to audit your business and rank every AI opportunity by impact, ease and risk. You'll write business prompts that produce usable work, backed by a brand voice guide and a team prompt library.

You'll use AI across marketing, sales, customer service and operations, with human review built in. And you'll protect customer data and your reputation with clear data rules and an AI usage policy.

All of it is built around one principle: AI drafts, people decide.

### 4. How the course is organized · 1:30

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "1 The AI Opportunity Audit · 2 Prompting for Business · 3 Marketing & Content"]**

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "4 Sales & Customer Service · 5 Operations & Admin · 6 Rollout, Safety & ROI"]**

There are six modules. We start with The AI Opportunity Audit, then Prompting for Business. Next come three modules where you put AI to work: Marketing and Content, Sales and Customer Service, and Operations and Admin.

We finish with Rollout, Safety and ROI. That module ends with a week-by-week 30-day rollout plan, so you finish with your first three AI projects running, not just a notebook full of ideas.

### 5. How to get the most from each lesson · 2:00

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Watch · Read · Do"]**

Each lesson works best in three steps. Watch the video for the core idea and a live example. Then read the written lesson, which has the full prompts, templates, worksheets and checklists.

Most important, do the exercise at the end of every lesson on your own business, with an AI assistant open beside you. If you're taking the course on the Principe AI website, every lesson also has an AI tutor you can ask questions.

### 6. Let's begin · 2:35

**[ON CAMERA]**

So open a blank document, and let's get started. In Lesson 1, Where AI Actually Pays Off, you'll learn the four ways AI creates real value in a business, and how to spot them in your own work. I'll see you there.
